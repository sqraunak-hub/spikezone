Older Industrial Stock: Clearing Is the Job

Budget for the clearing, not just the metres

Ghaziabad's industrial belt — Sahibabad, Loni, Meerut Road, Bulandshahr Road — contains a lot of sheds that have been standing for decades. On buildings like that, birds have not been present for a season. They have been present for years.

What that means in practice:

  • Accumulated droppings and nesting material on every truss, girder and tray. Clearing that is dusty, slow work and it comes before anything is fitted.
  • The debris falls, so sheeting below is not optional — especially over stock or machinery.
  • Nothing bonds to a fouled surface. Fitting over old deposits is why installations on old sheds fail.
  • Expect to find damage. Corroded sheeting, blocked gutters and nesting material packed against cabling usually turn up during clearing.
  • Nests are likely. Most wild birds in India are protected — check before clearing, and wait if there are chicks.

A quote that covers only the running metres of product on a shed like this is not a real quote.

What Ghaziabad Does to an Installation

  • Heavy dust and industrial grime — an adhesion problem before it is anything else. Clean and dry properly.
  • 45°C summers and hard UV destroy unstabilised plastic. This is the specification point to insist on.
  • Wide temperature swing works marginal fixings loose over seasons.
  • Fit before monsoon, while substrates are dry.
  • Old masonry and corroded steel need checking before anything is fixed to them.
