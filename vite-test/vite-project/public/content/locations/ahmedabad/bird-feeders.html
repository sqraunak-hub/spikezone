October to May. Nothing bonds reliably to damp masonry, which is the main reason installations fail early.

Will it attract pigeons to my building?

No. The ports and perches are sized for small birds, so sparrows and munias can use it and pigeons cannot. What attracts pigeons is loose grain scattered on a balcony or terrace — that is the practice to stop.
