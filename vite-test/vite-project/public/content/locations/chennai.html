The Calendar Is the Whole Point

Chennai is the exception on this list

Most of India does this work between October and May, because the south-west monsoon occupies June to September. Chennai gets comparatively little from that monsoon and takes its main rainfall from the north-east monsoon in October to December — exactly the months a Mumbai or Delhi supplier would recommend.

Anything fixed to damp masonry fails, so the practical calendar here is:

Cyclone Wind Load

This is why the fixing method is not negotiable here

Chennai sits on a cyclone-exposed coast. Wind accelerates over a roof edge or parapet, and a run that is holding fine in ordinary weather is a different proposition in a storm.

An installation that lets go at that height does not merely stop working — sections come down into a street. On exposed edges, upper floors and anything above an entrance or pavement, mechanical fixing is the correct specification and adhesive alone is not.

It also rules out the improvised remedies: wire, string and pinned-down mesh along a roof edge come adrift, and they come adrift over the road.

Salt and ultraviolet at the same time is an unusual combination — inland cities get the sun without the salt, and cooler coastal places get the salt without this much sun. Chennai gets both, which is why material grade matters here more than the price difference between suppliers.

The Terrace, Not the Balcony

Chennai's residential stock leans more toward independent houses and low-rise blocks with usable flat terraces than toward the balcony-dominated high-rise pattern of Mumbai. That shifts the typical job:

  • Parapet runs and roof edges rather than balcony netting — see rooftop jobs.
  • Overhead water tanks, which are a priority because of what sits under the droppings.
  • Terrace-mounted solar, increasingly common — and the gap under an array is the best nesting cavity on any roof. See solar panel bird control.
  • Sunshades and chajjas over windows, which are usually the actual perch rather than the sill below.
  • Compound wall copings and gate pillars.

And because you reach a terrace by walking up the stairs, this is the cheapest serious bird control work on any Chennai building — worth doing every surface in one visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should this work be done in Chennai?

February to September. Chennai's main rain is the north-east monsoon in October to December, which is the opposite of most of India — so advice written for Mumbai or Delhi gets the months exactly backwards here.

Adhesive or mechanical fixing?

Mechanical on every exposed edge, parapet and upper floor. This is a cyclone-exposed coast, and anything that lets go at height comes down into a street.

Will metal spikes rust here?

Poor-grade metal will, and it streaks rust down the wall. Ours are polycarbonate with no metal in them, which is the right specification for a salt-air coast.

Our house has a terrace rather than balconies.

Then it is a parapet, water tank and sunshade job rather than a netting one. It is also the cheapest kind of work on any building, because you reach it by the stairs — so do every surface in one visit.

We have solar on the terrace.

Include it. The gap under an array is the best nesting cavity on any roof, and the cost of ignoring it shows up in your generation figures.

Birds are on the sunshade, not the sill.

That is common here. The sill is just catching what falls, so spiking it alone changes nothing — the sunshade is what needs treating.

Do you supply the industrial belts?

Yes — Ambattur, Sriperumbudur, Oragadam and Maraimalai Nagar are regular project work: warehouse netting, trusses and cable trays.
