Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply Bird Feeders in Bangalore?

Yes. We manufacture in Delhi and despatch to Bangalore by courier or transport depending on the quantity.

What is the price?

Quoted by the unit. Send the measurement and a photograph and you will have a price the same day — it depends on quantity, so a rough figure is enough to start.

When is the best time to fit in Bangalore?

December to March, otherwise by forecast. Nothing bonds reliably to damp masonry, which is the main reason installations fail early.

Is there a minimum order?

Small orders are welcome. We would rather supply a metre than have a problem left unfixed because it felt too small to ask about.

Do you offer dealer or wholesale terms in Bangalore?

Yes — for contractors, hardware traders and pest control firms. Tell us the products and volumes you expect to move.

Will it attract pigeons to my building?

No. The ports and perches are sized for small birds, so sparrows and munias can use it and pigeons cannot. What attracts pigeons is loose grain scattered on a balcony or terrace — that is the practice to stop.
