Hot Process Areas

The one specification question specific to Faridabad

Auto component plants, forging shops and foundries have surfaces that run hot — duct runs, pipe racks, structures near furnaces and heat-treatment lines. Anywhere birds perch above those, the deterrent has to survive the temperature.

Our material is rated to 150°C. That comfortably covers ordinary plant steelwork, cable trays and lighting, and a great deal of ducting. It does not cover everything in a forging or foundry environment.

So the practical instruction is simple: take a surface temperature reading on any run near a heat source before it goes into the schedule, and tell us the figure. It is a five-minute check that prevents a material choice that quietly fails.

Working Inside a Plant

Faridabad's industrial estates are older and denser than Noida's, and the work has to fit around production rather than the other way round.

  • Permit to work governs height access, electrical isolation and hot work adjacency. Build the time for it into the programme.
  • Crane and gantry travel decides which face of a girder can be treated.
  • Nothing over running machinery, and nothing that can drop into a process.
  • Clearing comes first, and on older estates that is a significant part of the labour on its own.
  • Survey before the shutdown, not during it — see factory jobs for how the schedule of items is built.

The quantity almost always comes out higher than a first estimate, because of the doubling factors: both girder flanges, both cable tray rails, two rows on a wide parapet. On a typical hall those account for more than half the total.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can it be fitted near hot process equipment?

Up to 150°C, yes. Take a surface temperature reading on any run near a heat source before specifying and tell us the figure — forging and foundry environments can exceed it.

Is it safe on cable trays and control wiring?

Yes. It is non-conductive, so no interference with electrical or communication transmission, and it is flame retardant.

Can the work be done without stopping production?

Roof, parapet and perimeter work usually can. Anything over running machinery cannot, which is why internal items get scheduled into a planned shutdown.

We have monkeys, not birds.

Then you need the monkey product — taller, wider, mechanically fixed. Bird spikes are the wrong scale and a macaque simply steps over them.

Will monkey spikes clear our plant of monkeys?

No, and nobody should claim it. They block the specific route you treat. What keeps monkeys on an industrial estate is food — canteen waste and open bins — and that has to be dealt with alongside.

Can the monkeys be trapped or relocated?

Not by you or by a private contractor. They are protected wildlife and that is a Forest Department matter.

Do you quote in a format our purchase department can process?

Yes — line-item quantities, material specification including temperature rating, scope boundaries and programme.

Plant Scope, or a Monkey Route?

For a plant, send hall dimensions, girder and tray run lengths, fitting counts and your shutdown dates — plus a temperature reading on anything near a heat source. For monkeys, tell us where they actually walk and how long that stretch is.
