Carved Timber Is a Different Problem From Carved Stone

On wood, the damage is damp rather than acid

The walled city's pol houses carry some of the finest carved wooden facades in India — brackets, balcony fronts, window surrounds, column heads. Birds use every one of those projections, and what follows is not the same as on a sandstone facade.

On stone, droppings etch the surface and the carving erodes. On timber, the mechanism is moisture. Droppings and packed nesting material hold damp against the wood, and sustained damp in seasoned timber leads to rot and creates conditions for insect attack. The surface may look merely dirty long after the material underneath has started going.

Practical consequences:

  • Clearing matters more than on stone. Getting the material off the timber is the first and most valuable action.
  • Do not pressure wash carved wood. It drives water into the grain and into the joints.
  • Never drill the carving. Fix to a plain surface, or use a reversible method that does not penetrate.
  • Get the timber inspected if material has been sitting on it for years. There may be a conservation job under the bird job.

The transparent polycarbonate helps here for the same reason it helps in Jaipur — no colour to sit against the woodwork, and it lifts off in about a minute. On protected buildings, obtain written consent before anything is attached.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do bird droppings damage carved wood?

Yes, but differently from stone. On timber the problem is trapped moisture — droppings and nesting material hold damp against the wood, which invites rot and insect attack. The surface can look merely dirty long after the timber has started going.

Can we pressure wash the woodwork clean?

No. Pressure washing drives water into the grain and the joints, which is the opposite of what carved timber needs. Take conservation advice on the cleaning method.

Can spikes be fitted to a carved wooden facade?

Yes, but not through the carving. Fix to a plain surface or use a reversible method that does not penetrate the ornament, and get written consent first on a protected building.

Why do bird spikes fail so fast in Ahmedabad?

Heat and ultraviolet. On a west-facing elevation here, unstabilised plastic can turn brittle within a single summer. If a supplier cannot confirm UV stabilisation, assume it is absent.

When should the work be done?

October to May. Avoid the monsoon, and on a really hot day work early rather than at midday — both for the fitters and for the fixing.

Do you supply the Sanand and Vatva industrial belts?

Yes — roof void netting, opening closure, trusses, girders and cable trays in project quantities.

Our society is on SG Highway. Can you quote the whole tower?

Yes. Send one balcony opening size and the flat count — the per-flat figure at tower quantity is usually what gets it approved.
