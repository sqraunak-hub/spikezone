Pharma Sites: What the Inspection Actually Wants

Bird proofing is a documented control, not housekeeping

On a food warehouse, bird control is judged on whether birds are present. On a pharmaceutical site it is judged on whether you can demonstrate a control — which means an inspector wants to see the specification and the records, not just a clean ceiling.

What a quality or engineering head should be asking for at quotation stage:

  • A written scope naming every area covered, and explicitly what is excluded.
  • Material specification — mesh size and material, and for spikes the polycarbonate grade, non-conductive and flame retardant properties, and temperature rating.
  • Dated before-and-after photographs per area, which is what closes a finding.
  • A completion date that lands before the audit or the CAPA deadline.
  • A phasing plan that works around controlled and classified areas, because “we will shut the block” is not an available answer.
  • A cleaning and inspection interval you can put in a schedule.

Ask for all of that up front rather than after the work. Retro-fitting documentation to a completed job is far harder than issuing it alongside.

The physical work itself is the same as any plant — roof void netting, opening closure, girders, cable trays, light fittings. See factory jobs for how the schedule of items is built, and warehouse jobs for the netting arithmetic.

Granite Changes the Fixing

Hyderabad sits on granite, and it builds with it. A lot of the older and mid-range stock uses dense stone rather than brick and render, and that is worth flagging to whoever fits.

  • It holds a mechanical fixing extremely well once drilled — better than most substrates in India.
  • But it drills slowly, and needs the right bit. A fitter expecting plaster will struggle.
  • Adhesive works well on sound dressed stone, provided it is genuinely clean — dense stone gives a good surface.
  • Weathered rubble and old lime pointing are different — check before assuming.
  • On heritage stonework, use a reversible fixing and get consent first — see decorative mouldings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a pharma audit expect from bird proofing?

Evidence of a control, not just a clean site: written scope, material specification, dated before-and-after photographs, a completion date and an inspection interval. Ask for all of it at quotation stage.

What mesh size for a controlled area?

Specify from the smallest bird present — roughly 19 to 25 mm where sparrows or mynas are getting in. A small bird inside a classified area is the same finding as a pigeon.

Can it be fitted without shutting the block?

Usually yes, phased area by area. Roof and perimeter work often needs no interruption at all; internal work over process equipment is scheduled into a planned window.

Is the material safe near electrical and control wiring?

Yes — non-conductive and flame retardant, rated to 150°C. That is what makes it specifiable on cable trays and light fittings.

Our building is granite. Does that matter?

It holds mechanical fixings very well but drills slowly, so tell the fitter what the substrate is before they arrive. Adhesive works well on sound dressed stone provided it is genuinely clean.

When should the work be done?

October to May. Hyderabad is dry for most of the year, which is why adhesive performs better here than on the coasts — but nothing bonds during the monsoon.

Do you supply Genome Valley and Patancheru?

Yes, in project quantities and with documentation in a form your quality department can file.
