The mesh mistake

The commonest wasted purchase in Bangalore is 50 mm netting fitted against a myna or bulbul problem. It is the right mesh for pigeons and the wrong mesh for everything smaller, and the birds simply pass through it.

Specify from the smallest bird present, not the most obvious one. Send us a photograph of the bird if you are unsure — it is a five-second answer and it saves a whole order.

Mild Climate, Different Failure Mode

Bangalore is the easiest climate in India for an outdoor installation, and that has an unexpected consequence.

  • No salt-laden air, so corrosion is not the driver it is in Chennai or Mumbai.
  • No 45°C summers, so ultraviolet and thermal cycling do far less damage than in Delhi or Ahmedabad.
  • No cyclone wind load, so edge runs are under less stress.
  • Which means poor product survives longer here — long enough that buyers conclude the cheap option was fine.
  • So the failure, when it comes, is the fixing: adhesive applied to a dusty or damp surface, or a marginal fixing at height.

Practical version: in Bangalore, spend your attention on preparation and fixing method rather than agonising over material grade. Clean and dry properly, and fix mechanically at height and above walkways.

The Working Window

Bangalore does not have one long monsoon and one long dry season — rain is spread across more months, with peaks around June to July and again around September to October. The consequence is that the working window is scattered rather than seasonal.

  • December to March is the most reliably dry stretch.
  • Otherwise, work to the forecast rather than the calendar — the substrate needs to be dry when the fixing goes on and to stay dry while it cures.
  • Do not fit onto a wall that rained yesterday, however dry it looks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which mesh size do I need?

Around 50 mm if it is definitely pigeons. Roughly 19 to 25 mm if mynas, bulbuls or sparrows are involved — and in Bangalore they often are. Specify from the smallest bird present.

We netted the balcony and small birds still get in.

Wrong mesh. A 50 mm net is sized for pigeons; anything smaller passes straight through it. That is the commonest wasted purchase in this city.

Something is nesting inside our vent, not on a ledge.

That will be a myna or a parakeet — both are cavity nesters. Spikes do nothing there; the opening has to be closed, and not while there are chicks inside.

Do we need the expensive material in Bangalore?

The climate here is gentle enough that material grade shows up more slowly than in Delhi or Chennai. What still fails is the fixing — put your attention on cleaning, drying and using mechanical fixing at height.

When is the best time to fit?

December to March is the most reliably dry stretch. Otherwise work to the forecast rather than the season — the wall must be dry when the fixing goes on and while it cures.

Crows are pulling at what we fitted.

Crows do that, and they are heavier than pigeons. Use the taller, wider profile and fix mechanically — no loose ends for them to work at.

Do you supply Peenya and Bommasandra?

Yes — warehouse netting, trusses and cable trays, in project quantities.
