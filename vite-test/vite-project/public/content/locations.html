Why Location Changes the Specification

The same row of spikes fitted in Chennai and in Chandigarh is doing the same job against the same birds, but it is being attacked by completely different things. This is the part worth getting right before you order, because it is what decides whether the installation lasts three years or ten.

ConditionCitiesWhat it attacksWhat to specify
Salt-laden coastal air Mumbai, Chennai Metal fixings and any metal in the product. Rust streaking down the elevation follows within a season or two. Polycarbonate rather than metal; stainless or coated fixings only
Extreme UV and 45°C+ summers Delhi, Jaipur, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad, Lucknow Unstabilised plastic. It yellows, turns brittle and snaps at the pin roots. UV-stabilised material — this is the single biggest cause of premature failure inland
Heavy monsoon and humidity Mumbai, Kolkata, Pune, Chennai Adhesive fixings, and anything that traps water against masonry. Mechanical fixing; fit before monsoon, not during it
Cyclone-season wind load Chennai, Kolkata Edge and parapet runs. Wind accelerates over an exposed edge and lifts anything marginal. Mechanical fixing on all exposed edges, no exceptions
Dust and airborne grime Delhi, Ghaziabad, Faridabad, Noida Adhesion. The base sticks to the deposit, and the deposit lets go of the wall. Proper cleaning before fitting — the step most often skipped
Monkey pressure alongside birds Jaipur, Chandigarh The installation itself. Monkeys grip and test what birds only sit on. Monkey spikes, mechanically fixed

Our polycarbonate is UV stabilised and carries no metal to corrode, which covers the two biggest failure modes on that list. The one that stays your decision is the fixing method — and on coastal and cyclone-exposed elevations it should be mechanical.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply outside Delhi?

Yes, anywhere in India. We manufacture in Delhi and despatch by courier or transport depending on the quantity, to site or to your contractor.

Does my city change what I should order?

The product usually stays the same; the fixing method sometimes changes. On coastal and cyclone-exposed elevations — Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata — mechanical fixing is the right call rather than adhesive alone.

Why does UV matter so much in north India?

Because unstabilised plastic is what fails first in a Delhi or Jaipur summer — it yellows, embrittles and snaps at the pin roots, often within two seasons on a south-facing edge. Ours is UV stabilised, which is the property to check whoever you buy from.

Do you have offices in every city?

No, and we would rather say so. We are a manufacturer in Delhi supplying nationwide. That means direct pricing and a straight answer on the material, not a branch on your street.

Can you deliver to my contractor rather than to me?

Yes, and for industrial and project work that is usually the simplest arrangement.

Do you offer dealer or wholesale terms?

Yes, pan-India. Tell us the products and volumes you expect to move.
