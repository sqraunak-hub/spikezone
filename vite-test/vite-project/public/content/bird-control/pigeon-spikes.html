What Are Pigeon Spikes?

Pigeon spikes are strips of blunt-tipped pins or cones fixed along a surface so a pigeon cannot get a stable footing on it. The bird lands, finds nowhere to settle, and moves on. Nothing traps it, shocks it or harms it.

Pigeons are creatures of habit, and that is the real problem. Once a pair decides your balcony grill or the top of your AC outdoor unit is a safe place to nest, they return to it every season — and the droppings, feathers and nesting material come with them. Spikes break the habit at the surface rather than chasing the birds.

Pigeon spikes or bird spikes?

They are the same technology at two scales. Bird spikes have a narrow base for small surfaces — a sill, a pillar top, a moulding. Pigeon spikes are about coverage: wider ledges, longer runs and full balconies, where row count and total metres decide the job.

Pigeon Spike Installation

This is a DIY job. A standard balcony takes about an hour, and every kit ships with an illustrated guide in Hindi and English.

  1. Inspect the whole area. Note every surface the birds use — railing, the sill above it, the AC unit, the chajja. Treating one at a time just moves them.
  2. Remove the nest and clean. Clear nesting material and wash off the droppings. Pigeons navigate back to the smell, and adhesive will not bond to a soiled surface.
  3. Measure the running length. Total feet, plus about 10% for corners and overlaps. Ledges over four inches wide need two rows.
  4. Dry-lay and cut. Lay the strips out first and cut to length with a hacksaw so each run finishes flush at the corner.
  5. Fix them down. Peel the backing or run a continuous bead of silicone, then press firmly along the whole base. Use adhesive on AC units so the casing is not pierced, and keep the vents clear.
  6. Check for gaps. Sight along the run at eye level. Fill the last few inches with an offcut rather than leaving them open.

Expect two to three weeks of testing

A pair that has nested in the same spot for years will keep coming back to check it before giving up. That is normal. Do not pull the spikes off early — that is the one thing that guarantees they return.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do pigeon spikes work?

They take away the flat footing a pigeon needs to land and settle, so the bird chooses somewhere else. It is a physical deterrent at that surface, not a repellent that covers an area.

Are pigeon spikes safe for birds?

Yes. Tips are blunt and nothing traps, shocks or poisons the bird. This is why spikes are accepted in residential societies, hospitals and on heritage buildings.

Are they legal in residential buildings?

Yes. Spikes are a passive, non-lethal deterrent and are permitted in residential and commercial buildings across India, including rented flats and society balconies.

Will they work if pigeons already nest on my balcony?

Yes, provided you remove the nest and clean the area before fitting. An established pair may keep testing the spot for two to three weeks before relocating.

Can I fit spikes on my AC outdoor unit?

Yes, and it is one of the most common requests we get. Use adhesive rather than screws so the casing is not pierced, and keep the airflow vents clear.

How many strips will I need?

Divide the total running length in feet by the strip length and add about 10% for corners. A standard balcony railing is usually 10 to 15 ft, which the 20 ft combo pack covers with some spare.

Do spikes damage the wall or railing?

Adhesive-fixed strips leave only a silicone residue that scrapes off. Screw-fixed strips leave small holes, so use adhesive on rented or finished surfaces.

Do you provide installation?

Our products are made for DIY fitting and every kit includes a step-by-step guide in Hindi and English. For large or high-access sites, send us the details and we will advise on the right fitting method.

How long do they last?

Steel strips last many years outdoors. ABS cone spikes are UV-stable and typically last several seasons in direct sun before needing replacement.
